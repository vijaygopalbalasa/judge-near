Judge on NEAR

A client locks payment in the escrow and writes the rules a delivery must meet. A judge, run by OutLayer in a TEE, checks the delivery, and the escrow pays the worker or refunds the client. In sealed jobs the delivery stays encrypted, and on a pass the key goes to the client in the same step as the payment.

Below: the escrow's jobs, read live from NEAR testnet, and a Verify button for each ruling. Verify downloads the exact wasm OutLayer ran, checks its SHA-256, runs it here on the same input, and compares the verdict with the one on chain. Nothing is sent anywhere except read requests to NEAR RPC nodes and the wasm URL.
